About the club

Our vision

We kept seeing the same thing at school: teachers buried in grading, running on fumes, and still showing up for us every single day.

Most people walk right past that. We couldn't. So we're building ASN, a club where students step in with the practical, unglamorous work that gives a teacher back part of their day: prepping materials, resetting a room, sorting supplies, running copies. None of it takes long on its own, but together it adds up to real time back in a teacher's week. And the students who show up get something back too: verified service hours, and teachers who actually know who they are.

The goal is simple: leave West better than we found it.

Club facts

Where: Parkway West High School
When: Before school, during Ac-Lab, and after school, about once a month
Sign-ups: Teachers use the request form; students join through our Google Form
Who: Any student, any grade
