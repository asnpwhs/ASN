How it works

Students show up for teachers, and the hours they put in get verified. Here's how it plays out.

What we do

Where students step in

Every teacher has stacks of work to get through and not enough time in the day. That's the gap we help close.

📚

Classroom support

Sign up to help a teacher before school, during Ac-Lab, or after school: setting up the room, prepping materials, organizing supplies, running copies, and handling deliveries. Small tasks for you that give a teacher back a real part of their day.

💛

Fundraisers

We raise funds so classrooms are stocked with the supplies they need, easing a cost teachers often cover themselves.

The steps

How it plays out

For teachers → get a hand

1

Fill out the request form. Tell us what you need and which dates work for you: before school, during Ac-Lab, or after school.

2

We show up. Point us at the to-do list and consider it handled.

For students → get involved

1

Pick your spots. Sign up for the teachers and dates that work for you, on your schedule.

2

Get something back. Service hours, new people to meet, and positive relationships with teachers.

FAQ

Stuff you're probably wondering

Got a different question? Email us.

Who can join?

Anyone at West, any grade. There's a short application on our Google Form, but no experience and no tryouts.

How much time is this, really?

About one meeting a month, plus whatever extra you choose to take. It's built to fit around sports, jobs, and everything else you've got going on.

I'm a teacher. How do I get help?

Use the calendar page. Click "Request Help" and tell us what you need and which dates work: before school, during Ac-Lab, or after school. Students will then sign up to fill your session.

Is ASN official?

We're launching for the 2026–27 school year. So early members aren't just joining a club, they're deciding what it becomes.
